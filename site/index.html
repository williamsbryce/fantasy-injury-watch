Your inbox

Email notification rules

Thursday summary
5:00 p.m. onward. One scheduled injury digest per day if email is enabled and delivery succeeds.
Sunday summary
10:30 a.m. onward. One scheduled injury digest per day if email is enabled and delivery succeeds.
New urgent injury
An email is triggered when an MFL injury-monitoring run detects a newly urgent Out/Inactive-type status. This is checked at the next scheduled poll, not instantly when news breaks.
Automatic swap
A separate email after a lineup substitution is verified on MFL, identifying the players moved and the replacement's recent scoring.
Unable to resolve
An action-required email if an Out starter has no safe replacement, or if authentication, lineup validation, submission or readback cannot be confirmed.

Routine injury summary emails and the automatic substitution emails are separate. No email is required when the lineup guard finds no Out/Inactive starters.

Your starting lineup

Automatic substitutions

Only the first Thursday and first Sunday NFL game windows are covered. The guard calculates kickoff times from the current schedule rather than using a fixed local hour.

Thursday check
One scheduled pregame evaluation, roughly 35 to under 95 minutes before the first Thursday kickoff.
Sunday check
One scheduled pregame evaluation, roughly 35 to under 95 minutes before the first Sunday kickoff. It may be earlier when international games are scheduled.
What can change
Only a confirmed Out or Inactive starter can be replaced. All unaffected starters stay in place. The guard does not add players to your roster or rearrange healthy starters.

Hourly GitHub schedule triggers only evaluate whether it is the right pregame window. They do not change your lineup hourly. Manual guard runs are read-only.

Replacement selection

Decision tree: who goes in?

  1. Is a currently selected starter confirmed Out or Inactive?

    No: leave the lineup unchanged. Questionable or Doubtful starters are not automatically removed.

  2. Is that starter's game still unlocked?

    No: do not attempt the swap; flag that starter as unresolved.

  3. Which bench players are eligible?

    Only players on your active roster, selectable in MFL, at the same position, and playing a game that has not started. Exclude bye weeks, IR/taxi and anyone Questionable, Doubtful, Out, Inactive, or with an unclear injury status.

  4. Rank by recent fantasy performance

    Calculate average CFFL fantasy points from the last three available completed weekly scoring records, looking back as far as six weeks. A player with fewer records uses those available; missing scores are not assumed to be zero. Highest average wins; ties favour more recorded games, then player ID.

  5. Is a safe replacement available?

    Yes: replace only that Out starter. No: leave that spot alone and email you. If another Out starter can be replaced, make that independent change.

  6. Has your lineup changed since it was checked?

    Yes: stop rather than overwrite your changes. No: submit the complete lineup through MFL's authenticated native form once.

  7. Did MFL save exactly the intended lineup?

    Read it back and verify all 17 starters. Email confirmed changes and any unresolved players. If verification fails, send an urgent warning instead of claiming success. Do not retry or reverse the write automatically.

Example: valid swap

Your starting RB is Out. Two healthy RBs remain eligible on your bench. The one with the higher recent CFFL points average is selected; your other 16 starters are preserved.

Example: partial solution

Your RB and LB are both Out, but only the RB has a healthy replacement. The RB is swapped, the LB is left alone, and the email identifies the unresolved LB.

Timing limitation: You chose one Thursday and one Sunday pregame check. A player ruled Out after the Sunday check can be missed. GitHub Actions schedules are best-effort, so execution can also be delayed. These checks use MFL injury statuses, not a guaranteed live feed of official NFL inactive announcements.
